# Diabetes and your heart: five things to do this month
**[Practice name] · [Doctor name]**

> **Demo sample for the FlocCare GTM assignment.** Not reviewed by a clinician and not for patient use. In production each handout is written at a 6th-grade reading level, translated, reviewed by the practice's clinicians, and branded with the practice's name.

People with diabetes are more likely to have heart disease. These five steps lower that risk.

1. **Know your A1C.** Ask your doctor for your number and your goal.
2. **Check your blood pressure.** Ask your doctor what your goal is. Write your readings down.
3. **Ask about your cholesterol.** Many people with diabetes take a statin. Ask if you should.
4. **Move every day.** A 10-minute walk after meals helps lower blood sugar.
5. **Take your medicines every day.** Ask your doctor before you stop any medicine.

## Bring to your next visit
- Your blood sugar and blood pressure logs
- A list of all your medicines

## Call 911 if
- You have chest pain or pressure, trouble breathing, or signs of a stroke (face drooping, arm weakness, trouble speaking). With diabetes, heart attack signs can be milder, like tiredness, nausea or shortness of breath.
