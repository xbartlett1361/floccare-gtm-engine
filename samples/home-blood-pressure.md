## Why check at home
Your blood pressure at home tells your doctor more than one reading in the office. It helps them see if your medicine is working.

## How to get a good reading
1. Sit still for 5 minutes first. No coffee, smoking or exercise for 30 minutes before.
2. Sit with your back supported and both feet flat on the floor.
3. Rest your arm on a table so the cuff is at heart level. Put the cuff on bare skin.
4. Take 2 readings, 1 minute apart. Write both down.
5. Check in the morning before medicine and again in the evening.

## Call the office if
- Your readings are higher than the number your doctor gave you, several days in a row.

## Call 911 if
- Your reading is 180/120 or higher **and** you have chest pain, shortness of breath, back pain, numbness or weakness, trouble seeing, or trouble speaking.
