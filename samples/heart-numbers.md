These three numbers tell you a lot about your heart. Ask your doctor what your goals are and write them here.

| Number | What it means | My number | My goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blood pressure | How hard blood pushes on your arteries | | |
| LDL cholesterol | The "bad" cholesterol that builds up in arteries | | |
| A1C (blood sugar) | Your average blood sugar over about 3 months | | |

## Things that help all three
- Move most days. A brisk 30-minute walk counts.
- Eat more vegetables, fruit, beans and whole grains. Eat less salt and fewer processed foods.
- Don't smoke.
- Take your medicines every day, even when you feel fine.
- Sleep 7 to 9 hours.

## Bring to every visit
- Your home blood pressure log
- A list of all your medicines and supplements

## Call 911 if
- You have chest pain or pressure, trouble breathing, or signs of a stroke (face drooping, arm weakness, trouble speaking).
