## What PAD is
PAD (peripheral artery disease) means the arteries in your legs are narrowed. Your leg muscles don't get enough blood when you walk, so they cramp or ache. The pain usually stops when you rest.

## Your walking plan
Walking is one of the best treatments for PAD. Ask your doctor if this plan is right for you.
1. Walk until the pain is moderate.
2. Stop and rest until it goes away.
3. Start walking again.
4. Keep going for about 30 minutes, at least 3 days a week.

## Call the office if
- You have a sore on your foot or leg that is not healing.
- Your leg pain gets worse or starts happening when you rest.

## Call 911 if
- Your leg suddenly becomes cold, pale, numb or very painful.
