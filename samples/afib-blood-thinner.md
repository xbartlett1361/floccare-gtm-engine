## What AFib is
AFib (atrial fibrillation) makes your heart beat in an uneven way. Blood can pool in the heart and form a clot. A clot that travels to the brain can cause a stroke.

## What your blood thinner does
It makes clots less likely. It lowers your chance of a stroke. It does not fix the uneven heartbeat.

## Take it the right way
- Take it every day, at the same time. Do not skip doses.
- Do not stop it on your own, even before a procedure or dental work. Ask your doctor first.
- Tell every doctor, dentist and pharmacist that you take a blood thinner.
- Ask before taking pain medicine like ibuprofen or aspirin, or any supplement.

## Call the office if
- You have bleeding that won't stop, nosebleeds, bleeding gums, or bruises you can't explain.
- You missed a dose and are not sure what to do.

## Call 911 if
- You see signs of a stroke: face drooping, arm weakness, trouble speaking.
- You vomit blood, have black or bloody stools, or hit your head hard.
